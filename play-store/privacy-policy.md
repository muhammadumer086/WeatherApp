# SkyCast Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

SkyCast is a weather application provided by Invotyx. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information SkyCast uses

SkyCast may request access to your device's approximate and precise location so it can show weather for your current area. Location access is optional. You can deny or revoke it in your device settings and continue to search for cities manually.

When location access is granted, coordinates are sent over an encrypted connection to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather data. City or place searches are sent to Photon or Open-Meteo's geocoding service. These providers may process network information such as an IP address as part of delivering their services. Their own privacy policies govern their processing.

SkyCast stores your unit, time-format, appearance, and feels-like display preferences locally on your device. SkyCast does not require an account and does not include advertising or in-app analytics in version 1.0.0.

## Data retention

SkyCast does not operate an account database or retain your location on an Invotyx server. Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Third-party weather and geocoding providers may retain request data under their own policies.

## Data sharing

SkyCast sends only the information needed to provide weather and place-search results to the providers described above. SkyCast does not sell personal information.

## Security

Requests to weather and geocoding providers use HTTPS. No method of electronic transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Children's privacy

SkyCast is a general-audience weather utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
