## Full description

See the weather around you at a glance with SkyCast.

SkyCast combines current conditions, the next 24 hours, and a seven-day forecast in one calm, easy-to-read experience. Use your current location or search for cities around the world, then switch units and appearance to match the way you read weather.

Features:

- Current temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, UV, and visibility
- Hourly conditions and precipitation chance
- Seven-day high and low forecast
- Search for cities worldwide
- Celsius or Fahrenheit and km/h or mph
- 12-hour or 24-hour time
- Day, night, or automatic appearance
- Pull to refresh for the latest conditions

Location access is optional. If you do not grant it, SkyCast opens with Islamabad weather and city search remains available.

Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo. Place search also uses Photon. Forecasts can change; always follow official local warnings for severe weather.
